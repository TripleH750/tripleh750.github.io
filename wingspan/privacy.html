Wingspan Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026 (draft)

This Privacy Policy explains how Triple H Holdings ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Wingspan Android app (package com.mro.wingspan) (the "App"). It is meant to be read together with the Terms of Use.

1. The short version

Everything you enter stays on your device. There is no account and no cloud copy. We do not operate servers that receive your inventory, and the App has no analytics or advertising SDKs. Backup files and exports are created only when you choose, and you decide where to save or share them.

2. Information stored on your device

The App is a personal inventory and record-keeping tool. Information you enter, including photos, receipts, flight logs and registration numbers, is stored only on your device. We do not receive this information, and we cannot see, change, or recover it remotely.

3. No accounts, servers, analytics, or cloud sync

The App has no account system. We do not operate servers that receive your inventory. The App has no analytics or advertising SDKs, and it does not sync your data to the cloud.

4. Backups and exports

Backup files and CSV exports are created only when you choose, and you decide where to save or share them. Backup, export and restore stay free for everyone. We do not receive those files unless you choose to send them to us.

Treat backup files like valuables: they can contain photos, registration numbers and notes. Keep them somewhere private.

If your device is lost, reset or damaged and you have no backup file, your data cannot be recovered by us.

5. Photos

If you add photos or receipts, they stay on your device. Location data is stripped automatically when a photo is added. The App may ask for camera permission so you can take a picture. Choosing a photo from your gallery uses the system photo picker and does not require a photo-library permission. You can refuse the camera, and you can still use the App without photos.

6. App Lock

If you turn on App Lock, the App can use your device's fingerprint, face, or screen lock to unlock. That check happens on your device. We do not receive your biometric data.

7. Purchases

If you buy Wingspan Lifetime, the purchase is processed by Google Play and RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and a purchase receipt only, so it can confirm your unlock. It does not receive your inventory, photos, registration numbers, or backup files. Google Play may also process the payment under its own policy. Your inventory still stays on your device.

If you use the referral program, RevenueCat also stores your referral code and any friend’s code you enter, attached to that same anonymous app user ID. Nothing from your hangar is included.

7a. Reminders and notifications

If you enter an AMA expiration date and allow notifications, the App schedules local reminders on your device (30, 7 and 1 days before). They are created and delivered by your phone’s operating system. The App does not use push notification servers and does not request a push token.

8. Email

If you email us at triplehholdings.support@gmail.com, we receive only what you choose to send in that email. We use it only to answer you and do not add it to the App.

9. Other companies

Google Play, RevenueCat, your device maker, and the Android operating system may collect their own information under their own policies, for example about installs, payments, or device operation. That is separate from the inventory you enter in the App, which stays on your device.

10. Children

You must be at least 13 years old, or the age of majority where you live if that is older, to use the App. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. If the changes are material, we will update the effective date on this page and, where appropriate, provide notice in the App.

12. Contact

Questions or support requests: triplehholdings.support@gmail.com (Triple H Holdings).
