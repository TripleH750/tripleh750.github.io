Gun Locker Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026 (draft)

This Privacy Policy explains how Triple H Holdings ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Gun Locker Android app (package com.mro.gunlocker) (the "App"). It is meant to be read together with the Terms of Use.

1. The short version

Everything you enter stays on your device. There is no account and no cloud copy. We do not operate servers that receive your inventory, and the App has no analytics or advertising SDKs. Backup files and reports are created only when you choose, and you decide where to save or share them.

2. Information stored on your device

The App is a personal inventory and record-keeping tool. Information you enter, including photos, receipts and serial numbers, is stored only on your device. We do not receive this information, and we cannot see, change, or recover it remotely.

3. No accounts, servers, analytics, or cloud sync

The App has no account system. We do not operate servers that receive your inventory. The App has no analytics or advertising SDKs, and it does not sync your data to the cloud.

4. Backups and reports

Backup files and reports are created only when you choose, and you decide where to save or share them. We do not receive those files unless you choose to send them to us.

Treat backup files and reports like valuables: they can contain serial numbers and photos. Keep them somewhere private.

If your device is lost, reset or damaged and you have no backup file, your data cannot be recovered by us.

5. Photos

If you add photos or receipts, they stay on your device. Location data is stripped automatically when a photo is added. The App may ask for camera or photo-library permission so you can take or choose those pictures. You can refuse, and you can still use the App without photos.

6. App Lock

If you turn on App Lock, the App can use your device's fingerprint, face, or screen lock to unlock. That check happens on your device. We do not receive your biometric data.

7. Email

If you email us at triplehholdings.support@gmail.com, we receive only what you choose to send in that email. We use it only to answer you and do not add it to the App.

8. Other companies

Google Play, your device maker, and the Android operating system may collect their own information under their own policies, for example about installs or device operation. That is separate from the inventory you enter in the App, which stays on your device.

9. Children

You must be at least 18 years old, or the age of majority where you live if that is older, to use the App. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. If the changes are material, we will update the effective date on this page and, where appropriate, provide notice in the App.

11. Contact

Questions or support requests: triplehholdings.support@gmail.com (Triple H Holdings).
